Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CineQ. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our website (https://cineq.tech) and our Google OAuth login services.

2. Information We Collect

When you log in using your Google Account, we collect the following basic information provided by Google:

  • Email Address: To identify your account and send important service notifications.
  • Full Name: To personalize your user experience within the CineQ app.
  • Profile Picture URL: To display your avatar in your user profile.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information collected from Google APIs strictly to:

  • Create and manage your CineQ user account.
  • Provide the core functionality of our application.
  • Improve user-facing features and security.

4. Google API "Limited Use" Disclosure

CineQ's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • Third-Party Services: We do not share your Google user data with any third-party AI models or external marketing agencies.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data only if required by law or to protect the security of our users.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. If you wish to delete your data, you can do so within your account settings or by contacting us at support@cineq.tech. Upon request, we will delete all data associated with your Google identity within 30 days.
